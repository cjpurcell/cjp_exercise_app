Sunday cardio

Before week 1Running loads the proximal hamstring tendon in a lengthened position on every stride, which is a common aggravator of what you're dealing with. If you get deep aching at the sit bone during a run or in the 24 hours after, stop running and switch to incline treadmill walking (50–60 min at 10–12% grade), bike, or elliptical for the rest of the block. The aerobic benefit is identical.
